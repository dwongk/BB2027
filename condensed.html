About the two views
  • Expanded - the full view. Each category page lists every matching word in the book, one row per word, with the verses that contain it.
  • In Context - made mainly for the numbers and times categories. It turns number words into figures and lists the largest first, and it pulls in a little of the surrounding words for context. It was applied to every category, but numbers is where it really helps. Like Expanded, it groups the material by verse: each row is one verse (or group of verses) with the words from that category that appear in it.
